### Manual Checks

- [ ] Compare every matrix-marked high-relevance source family with the
  research log and person dossier for an explicit result or access incident.
- [ ] Review each new relationship independently against the cited source
  image; do not promote name matches alone.
- [ ] Confirm that no Bernhard–Arne evidence search was introduced.

### Risks

- Source-family scope can become unbounded; constrain execution to the fixed
  next-edition cohort and record deferred leads without following them deeper.
- Sparse or indexed records can suggest false identity matches; require a
  multi-field identity chain or retain `LEAD`/`CONFLICT` status.
- UI-only sources can fail outside authenticated sessions; surface the
  incident immediately and preserve a browser/manual reactivation route.

## Approach

Work breadth-first in small source-family batches across the fixed
next-edition cohort. First close central-spine gaps evenly across both sons'
lines, then apply the matrix-marked censuses, extracts, probate, tax/mantals,
court, parish/poor-relief and conditional occupational sources to build rich
portraits. Rotate across people and source families so an easy branch cannot
run a generation ahead. After each batch, synchronize dossiers, citations,
media, frontier and the coverage matrix. The final research checkpoint is an
owner gate, not authorization to create the next PDF.
