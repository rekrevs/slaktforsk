### Risks

- Equal depth can be confused with fabricated completeness. A documented
  unknown parent counts as an honest terminal slot; a name guess never does.
- Rich biography can become unbounded. Establish a complete core biography for
  the finite edition cohort before optional embellishment.
- The two living sibling roots and their parents require privacy-conscious
  presentation even in a private family artifact.

## Approach

Work breadth-first across the missing or weak wife-side slots first, because
Sverker's contribution already passes the matching bounded audit. Synchronize
the sibling-root representation and evidence model as each parent-bearing
record is secured. Then run a finite person-by-person biography audit and
produce an explicit edition manifest for T-0016.
