# T-0008: Restore pedigree audit integrity and close the depth-5 wave

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: M | **Phase**: COMPLETE

## Context

Each generational wave ends with a bounded audit. T-0007 was initially closed
after `ancestor-audit.mjs` reported zero unresolved tips, but a Unicode parser
bug was then found: `\w` did not recognize Swedish letters in `Föräldrar:` and
could attach parents in the wrong direction. The owner has therefore set a
continuous goal to restore audit integrity, close all ancestry through depth 5
strictly breadth-first, and register (but not research) the resulting depth-6
frontier.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Add regression coverage for Swedish prose labels and verify the derived pedigree against an independently reviewed expected structure.
- [x] Complete P-0095 from the located 1840 Bygdeå birth entry before working at depth 5.
- [x] Give every open depth-5 tip verified parents or a valid terminal status after relevant lawful online sources and identified providers have been checked.
- [x] Preserve all named people and relevant biographical facts in target records with full citations, images and checksums.
- [x] Register the resulting depth-6 frontier without researching it in this task.
- [x] Reconcile `genealogy/frontier.md`, the research log, the person records and Wotan with the same verified state.
- [x] Pass the corrected audit, the independent structural check and the full genealogy validator.

## Approach

Repair and regression-test the graph derivation first. Continue strictly at the
lowest unresolved depth: P-0095, then all depth-5 tips including any parents her
birth entry opens. Treat a provider-specific access failure as evidence about
that provider, not as proof that the record is unavailable everywhere. Stop the
research wave after registering depth 6.
