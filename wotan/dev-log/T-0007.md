# T-0007: Research the complete depth-3 ancestral wave and all encountered kin

**Status**: ONGOING | **Phase**: DOING | **Size**: L

## Context

After depth 2 is closed, research all verified depth-3 ancestors in a balanced
wave. Conditional lines may be investigated for identity and relationship
evidence but must remain visibly conditional.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] For every verified depth-3 ancestor, locate and cite birth/baptism, parents and relevant childhood household, or document the search gap.
- [ ] Establish each forward parent-child relation independently of same-name similarity.
- [ ] Capture spouses, every child, siblings, witnesses, sponsors and other explicitly related named people encountered in target records.
- [ ] Preserve relevant full source images locally with stable names and checksums.
- [ ] Record biographies, occupations, residences, migrations and name variants supported by the searched sources.
- [ ] Update the frontier with every candidate depth-4 ancestor and mark conditional or ended lines.
- [ ] Verify JSON, links, identifiers, image manifests/checksums and claim-to-citation coverage.

## Approach

Work one person to a documented checkpoint, then move across the generation so
that no easy line outruns a closer unresolved line without an explicit reason.
Use original church records as the core evidence and census/index/secondary
trees as finding aids and corroboration.

## Verification Plan

- Validate `wotan/backlog.json` as JSON and enforce dependency/status consistency.
- Check that every relative Markdown link resolves and every referenced P-, S- and C-id exists.
- Recompute checksums for locally preserved images and compare them with citation metadata.
- Manually audit each new parent-child link against the cited original record.
- Confirm that negative searches and unresolved identity conflicts are recorded.
